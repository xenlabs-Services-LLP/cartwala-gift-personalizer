CARTWALA V5 PSD AUTO TEMPLATE GUIDE

1. Choose the Shopify product in the Cartwala app.
2. Keep each customer-replaceable photo area in its own PSD layer or group.
3. Name those layers PHOTO_1, PHOTO_2, PHOTO_3 or UPLOAD_1, UPLOAD_2, and so on.
4. The PHOTO/UPLOAD layer transparency is used as that photo slot's exact mask.
5. Every visible Photoshop text layer becomes an editable customer text field automatically.
6. Prefix decorative/non-editable text layers with STATIC_ or LOCKED_.
7. Keep the product/background/frame design in normally named layers.
8. Upload the PSD in "PSD auto template import". Review the result and click Save configuration.

There is no fixed photo or text count. The importer supports up to 200 editable fields in one product.

Recommended PSD setup:
- Use RGB colour mode and 8-bit channels.
- Keep the canvas at the final artwork ratio.
- Use transparent PHOTO/UPLOAD shapes with clean edges.
- Rasterize unsupported Photoshop effects if their appearance must be exact.
- Upload any non-system font separately under Custom fonts if customers must see that exact font in the browser.
